Technologies

Two technology platforms, one development framework.

We approach biomethane and pyrolysis as separate infrastructure projects. Each technology requires its own input stream, technical solution and offtake model.

Renewable gas

Biomethane

Anaerobic digestion converts suitable organic inputs into biogas, which is then cleaned and upgraded to biomethane.

Organic inputs→Digestion→Biogas upgrading→Biomethane
Circular processing

Pyrolysis

A thermochemical process treats suitable carbon-based inputs under limited oxygen conditions, producing gaseous, liquid and solid outputs.

Input stream→Pre-treatment→Pyrolysis reactor→Usable outputs

Specific technology, suppliers and process parameters are selected only after assessing the site, input composition, target outputs and project economics.

Impact

Development focused on real assets and usable outputs.

We structure the portfolio so that each project addresses a specific local input stream, infrastructure need and offtake model. Biomethane, landfill gas and pyrolysis have different economics, but share one principle: converting an underused stream into a long-term energy or industrial product.

  • valorisation of organic and waste streams
  • projects based on physical infrastructure
  • preparation for strategic, equity and debt financing

Development Partner Opportunity

ZK nextgen energy is preparing a portfolio of renewable gas and circular infrastructure projects across Europe, Africa and Asia. The current pipeline includes biomethane in Poland, renewable and landfill gas in Senegal, and pyrolysis opportunities in Africa and Asia.

Each opportunity is developed towards an investable and bankable structure – from site and input streams through technical concept and permitting to offtake, SPV structuring and financing.

Project milestones

Professional development pathway

01

Site and infrastructure screening

Assessment of suitable locations, utilities, infrastructure access and key technical constraints.

02

Input-stream strategy

Identification and validation of feedstock, waste streams or other critical project inputs.

03

Technical concept

Preliminary technology layout, capacity design and EPC budget preparation.

04

Permitting and EIA

Environmental, planning and administrative preparation towards project approval.

05

Offtake and output markets

Preparation of commercial routes for gas, energy and other industrially usable outputs.

06

Project SPV and financing

Creation of a bankable project structure suitable for equity and debt financing.

07

Ready-to-build phase

Finalization of permits, contracts and financing documentation before construction.
